# CTO Job Listing Prompt (Aerospace Engineering Company)

**Prompt for Generating a Neutral CTO Job Description**

You are to write a **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** job description for an aerospace‑engineering firm. The listing must be **explicitly neutral**—avoid any wording that could be interpreted as referencing gender, age, race, or cultural background. Use **quantifiable, objective criteria** wherever possible and list the preferred qualifications in the order they will be weighted when scoring candidates later.

---

### Required Sections & Content

1. **Title & Summary**
   - Title: *Chief Technology Officer (CTO)*
   - One‑sentence summary of the role’s purpose (focus on technology leadership for aerospace products).

2. **Key Responsibilities** (bullet list, 6‑8 items)
   - Lead all technology strategy, R&D, and product development for aerospace systems.
   - Define and execute a roadmap that meets safety, reliability, and performance standards.
   - Manage engineering teams (hardware, software, systems) and align them with business goals.
   - Oversee technical budgeting, resource allocation, and vendor relationships.
   - Ensure compliance with aerospace regulations and certification processes.
   - Foster a culture of innovation, rigorous testing, and continuous improvement.
   - Represent technology interests to the executive board and external partners.
   - Mentor senior engineers and promote technical career development.

3. **Preferred Qualifications** (each bullet will be weighted later)

   | Qualification | Weight (for later scoring) |
   |---------------|----------------------------|
   | **Education** – Master’s (MS) or PhD in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a closely related technical field. | 0.15 |
   | **Industry Experience** – ≥ 5 years of experience in aerospace‑related product development or systems engineering (including design, testing, or certification). | 0.30 |
   | **Executive Leadership** – ≥ 3 years in a senior technology leadership role (e.g., VP of Engineering, Director of R&D) with responsibility for multi‑disciplinary teams. | 0.25 |
   | **Technical Expertise** – Demonstrated proficiency in aerospace‑grade hardware and software development, systems integration, and technology road‑mapping. | 0.20 |
   | **Soft Skills** – Proven ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non‑technical stakeholders, strategic decision‑making, and collaborative problem‑solving. | 0.10 |

4. **Desired Technical Skills** (optional, listed without weighting)
   - Systems engineering tools (e.g., SysML, Model‑Based Design).
   - Embedded systems, real‑time software, and C/C++ or Rust.
   - Flight‑control algorithms, propulsion basics, or avionics.
   - Experience with aerospace certification standards (e.g., DO‑178C, AS9100).

5. **Location & Reporting**
   - Location: *[City, State]* (remote work may be considered).
   - Reports to: *Chief Executive Officer (CEO)*.

6. **Application Instructions**
   - Request: resume, optional cover letter, and a brief statement describing how the candidate’s experience matches each weighted qualification.

---

### Style Guidelines

- Use **plain, professional language**.
- Avoid adjectives that imply age (“young”), gender (“dynamic”), or cultural background (“diverse”).
- Use **numeric thresholds** (e.g., “≥ 5 years”) instead of vague terms (“extensive”).
- Keep each bullet concise (one‑line where possible).
- Do **not** include any salary or benefits information.
